# Services pricing shortcuts

## Goal
Add two low-key ways to reach the existing pricing section without changing the Services page order or competing with its main calls to action.

## Changes
- Add a muted, underlined “↓ Just here for pricing?” link below the two Services hero buttons.
- Add a compact fixed “Pricing” tab with a price-tag icon after the hero leaves view; keep it separated from the existing WhatsApp and back-to-top controls.
- Smooth-scroll both shortcuts to the exact pricing heading and briefly pulse that heading on arrival.
- Keep the shortcut exclusive to the Services page and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Verification
Check the Services page on mobile, tablet, and desktop; confirm visibility timing, spacing, smooth scrolling, heading highlight, and existing floating controls.
