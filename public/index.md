# AB Digital Consultancy

A website is only the beginning.

Most web developers hand you a website and disappear. We stay: hosting it, updating it, and picking up the phone when something breaks. We work with small and medium businesses from Bengaluru, Karnataka, and anywhere in India.

## What we do

One partner for everything online, and for the things you print.

- **Web design & development:** We design and build the site ourselves. No templates picked off a shelf, no page builder mess left behind.
- **Hosting & domain management:** Your site has to stay up. We handle the boring part so you never think about it.
- **CMS & content updates:** New prices, new photos, a new branch. Send it across and it goes live.
- **SEO & local visibility:** Most of your customers search before they call. We make sure they find you first.
- **Ongoing maintenance:** Websites decay quietly. Ours do not, because someone is looking after them.
- **Digital marketing support:** Only when it makes sense for you. We would rather say no than sell you ads you do not need.
- **Flyers & print materials:** The same care, offline. Your website and your shop signage should look like the same business.

We also offer [ISO certification consulting](https://abdigitalconsultancy.in/iso-consultancy.md).
