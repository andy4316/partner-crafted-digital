# About AB Digital Consultancy

It's the relationship, not the transaction. A website isn't a product you take delivery of once. It's a working part of the business, and it needs someone standing behind it the day after launch and the year after that.

AB Digital started from a repeated complaint, not a business plan. Small business owners kept describing the same experience: a website built once, then abandoned, by someone impossible to reach the moment something broke. This was built to be the opposite of that: one person, answerable, for as long as the site needs to keep working.

## The mark

Our logo is two pillars with an arrow in the space between them. One pillar is your business, already running. The other is us, the side that stays. The arrow only exists because both stand there.

## How we work

1. **We say no more than we say yes.** If something doesn't help your customers, we won't build it just because it's billable.
2. **Nothing ships without you seeing it first.** No surprise launches.
3. **Ownership is the default.** Your domain, your code, your data, from day one.
4. **We stay after launch.** Updates, hosting, backups and search health continue while you run the business.

## What we've decided never to do

- Sell you ads you don't need, just to pad an invoice
- Lock your site behind a platform you can't leave
- Disappear the day your site goes live
- Pretend a template is a custom build
