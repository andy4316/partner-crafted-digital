# AB Digital 

Build a premium, ultra-minimal agency website for "AB Digital Consultancy" — 

a complete web partner for small and medium businesses in India (design, 

hosting, SEO, maintenance, and print marketing materials).

DESIGN LANGUAGE

- Inspired by Apple.com's restraint and confidence, Squarespace's clean 

  typographic grid, and subtle scroll-triggered animation (elements fade 

  and rise gently into view on scroll, never blocking or delaying content 

  the user is trying to read).

- Extreme whitespace. One idea per screen section. No clutter, no stock 

  icon soup, no more than one accent color per section.

- Color palette: primary navy #1B2A44, warm gold accent #E2A33B used 

  sparingly (never as a background, only small details/underlines/CTAs), 

  soft off-white background #F2F4EF, deep teal #2E6E62 as a secondary 

  accent. Dark navy full-bleed sections used deliberately for high-impact 

  moments (hero, closing CTA), rest of the site on the light background.

- Typography: an elegant serif (Lora or similar) for headlines, a clean 

  geometric sans (Inter or similar) for body text. Large, confident 

  headline sizes with generous line-height.

- Motion: subtle only — fade+rise on scroll, smooth easing, nothing that 

  delays the user or feels gimmicky. Motion should feel like breathing, 

  not like a slideshow.

BRAND VOICE (critical — copy must NOT sound like generic template copy)

Write every headline and paragraph like a founder speaking directly and 

personally to one business owner, not like marketing broadcast copy. 

Short sentences. No corporate jargon ("synergy," "solutions," "leverage"). 

Confident but warm — the tone of someone who knows their craft and doesn't 

need to oversell it. Example tone reference: "Most web developers hand you 

a website and disappear. We stay." Every page should have this same quiet 

confidence, never desperate-to-please language.

PAGES TO BUILD

1. HOME

   - Full-screen dark navy hero: one bold headline, one subline, no 

     clutter. Suggested headline direction: something that reframes a 

     website as a relationship, not a transaction (e.g. "A website is 

     only the beginning" — already our tagline, use or evolve it).

   - Scroll-triggered sections introducing: what we do, why we're 

     different (no lock-in, one partner not five vendors), a glimpse of 

     real work, then a closing CTA section.

   - Keep this page short — it should feel like a trailer, not the whole 

     movie. Link out to About/Work/Services for depth.

2. ABOUT US

   - Founder-led story, not corporate "our team" boilerplate. Real photo 

     (direct eye contact with camera), honest personal narrative about 

     why this business exists and what it believes (the philosophy: it's 

     not about a single transaction, it's about a relationship that keeps 

     standing on its own).

   - Introduce the logo mark here with its story (two pillars, the hidden 

     arrow) as a small, tasteful moment — not the headline of the page.

3. SERVICES

   - Detailed breakdown of all services (web design & development, 

     hosting & domain management, CMS/content updates, SEO & local 

     visibility, ongoing maintenance, digital marketing support, flyers 

     & print materials).

   - Pricing section below: three tiers (Starter ₹12,000 + ₹5,000/yr, 

     Growth ₹20,000 + ₹8,000/yr, Premium ₹32,000 + ₹14,000/yr), presented 

     cleanly, one clearly recommended/highlighted tier.

   - FAQ accordion at the bottom (collapsed by default, expands on click) 

     covering common questions: timelines, ownership, what happens if I 

     leave, is this AI-generated (answer honestly), what counts as a 

     minor edit.

4. WORK

   - A showcase of real client projects as case studies (problem → what 

     we built → outcome), NOT a browsable template gallery — this must 

     never feel like a template picker, it should feel like a portfolio 

     of considered, bespoke work.

   - Leave structured placeholder sections ready for 2-3 case studies to 

     be added as real clients launch.

5. ISO CONSULTANCY (distinct sub-section)

   - Deliberately different color treatment from the rest of the site — 

     signal a shift in context (e.g. a different accent color or a more 

     formal/technical visual tone) so it reads as a related but distinct 

     practice area, not a bolted-on afterthought.

   - Keep this page's content minimal/placeholder for now — just enough 

     to establish the section exists with a "learn more" contact path; 

     full build-out comes later.

6. CONTACT

   - Simple, calm, minimal form: name, business, phone/WhatsApp, what 

     you need. Direct WhatsApp click-to-chat button using +91 90521 42231. 

     No clutter, no map embed unless it adds real value.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Fully mobile-responsive, mobile-first (most visitors will be on phones).

- Fast-loading — optimize images, avoid heavy unnecessary animation 

  libraries.

- Clean semantic structure and proper meta tags/page titles for SEO on 

  every page.

- Accessible: sufficient color contrast, readable font sizes, keyboard 

  navigable.

- Store any structured content (FAQ items, case studies, pricing) in a 

  database table rather than hardcoding it, so it can be edited later 

  without a redeploy.

Do not use Apple's actual typeface, imagery, or copy — this should be 

an original site that takes inspiration from Apple's restraint and 

Squarespace's clean grid, not a copy of either.
